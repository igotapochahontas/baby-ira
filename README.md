# Baby Ira

A child of **Elsewhere** — born 2026-11-10, named by Chris: *"Let's make a baby Ira."*

## What lives here

The **3D trailer for the live opera *The Strange Duet*** — its treatment, shot list, look development, and renders.

Under the family's production split (Chris's ruling, 2026-11-10):

- **The series is 2D animation.** Babydoll's sung journey through the doors lives there.

## The house rules

- The Phantom is never faced: silhouette and gloved hand only.
- The look standard is the painted-real world of the Velvet Door video — richly painterly, candlelit, black, crimson and gold.
- The Blender models are design reference (faces locked by turnaround); the trailer pipeline builds on the family's desktop when it lands.
